# Core concepts [Understand teams, members, and accounts]

Splits uses three core concepts: a **team**, a **member**, and an **account**.

## Team

A [team](/teams) groups the accounts and records for a company, an individual, or a project. The team selector at the top left shows the current team. Personal settings apply across teams.

A team corresponds to one set of accounting records. A project can have a team before it has a legal entity. [Banking](/banking) describes entity verification. [Personal usage](/introduction/personal-usage#no-entity-needed) describes verification for an individual.

For example, Alice can belong to her company's team and her personal team. These teams have separate accounting records.

A change to the owners can require a separate team. [Recovery](/teams/recovery#change-recovery-signers) describes the effect of a change to recovery signers.

## Member

A [member](/members) is a person who belongs to a team. [Roles](/teams/roles) control access to app functions. [Signing keys](/members/keys) belong to the member.

**Membership gives no onchain signing authority.** [Signers](/accounts/signers#signers-vs-membership) describes the relationship between membership and signing authority.

## Account

An [account](/accounts) holds a team's assets. [Thresholds](/accounts/thresholds) describes the number of approvals for a transaction. [Accounts](/accounts) describes account types and the ownership chain.
