# Networks and assets [Networks at Full and Partial support, and asset types]

Splits runs on EVM networks at two support tiers. **Full**: all features available on this network. **Partial**: balances and sending work on this network; accounting history, swaps, bridging, and NFTs may not be available. The app tags Partial networks in [Settings > Networks](https://app.splits.org/settings/team/networks/), where Owners turn networks on and off per team (see [Settings](/teams/settings)).

## Full support

- Ethereum
- Base
- Optimism
- Arbitrum
- Polygon
- BSC
- Celo
- World Chain
- Tempo

These networks also work with the [browser extension](/introduction/extension) and in-app [WalletConnect](/integrations/walletconnect).

## Partial support

- Zora
- Linea
- Scroll
- Monad

Most EVM-equivalent networks can be listed at Partial support, pending our underlying providers: email support to request one.

## Gradual rollout

A network can launch gradually behind a feature flag: it is visible only to teams the flag is enabled for, and joins the lists above once fully rolled out. Currently in gradual rollout:

- HyperEVM (Full support)
- Robinhood Chain (Partial support)

## Enabling and disabling networks

A team's accounts hold balances and transact on the networks the team has enabled: **balances are shown for enabled networks only**. Team setup starts with Full networks; enable any listed network afterward in *Settings > Networks*. Toggling networks edits a draft selection; one save applies the whole change, with newly enabled networks added in a single transaction.

Enabled networks are kept in sync: an account has the same address, signers, threshold, and owner on all of them, because [signer changes are signed once for every active network](/accounts/editing#changing-signers-and-thresholds). An account's state is synced to a newly enabled network before the account is used there.

The same settings page shows assets detected on networks that are listed but not enabled; enable the network to see and use them. Disabling a network is a visibility change only: nothing happens onchain, and no balances or history are deleted.

## Sending to other networks

Sends and swaps can deliver tokens to an external address on networks beyond this page's lists (including Solana). Crosschain routes go through the [swap providers](/transactions/swaps); coverage follows [Relay's supported networks](https://docs.relay.link/references/api/api_resources/supported-chains).

## Assets

- Native tokens (e.g. ETH)
- ERC-20
- ERC-721
- ERC-1155

If you send an asset outside these types to a Splits account, email support and we will attempt to return it.

## Programmatic access

Via the [Splits CLI / MCP](/introduction/agents):

- `splits chains list`: every listed network with its tier and per-feature capability flags (**Read** scope)
- `splits chains get --chainId 8453`: one network's entry (**Read** scope)

The underlying endpoint is `GET /public/v1/chains` (API key with **Read** scope); see [Use the API](/introduction/agents#use-the-api). Networks in [gradual rollout](#gradual-rollout) are excluded until fully rolled out.
