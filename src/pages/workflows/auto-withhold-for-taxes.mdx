# Auto-withhold for taxes in USDC

*Last update 11 November 2025*

This guide explains how to set up an automated system that receives a variety of tokens, converts X% into USDC and sets aside for tax purposes, and forwards the remaining amount to a dedicated account.

<video controls src="/images/auto-withhold-for-taxes/vid-1.mp4" />

## Create new accounts

In Splits ([teams.splits.org](http://teams.splits.org)) create two accounts: **`Tax`** and **`Operating`**.

![](/images/auto-withhold-for-taxes/img-1.gif)

## Create a [Diversifier](https://docs.splits.org/templates/diversifier)

In the Splits Explorer, [create a new Diversifier on Base](https://app.splits.org/new/diversifier/?chainId=8453). Use the following parameters:

- **Destination 1**<br />Share of income: 40%<br />Output token: USDC<br />Beneficiary: the address of the **`Tax`** account you created in step 1
- **Destination 2**<br />Share of income: 60%<br />Output token: forward as is<br />Beneficiary: the address of the **`Operating`** account you created in step 1
- **Owner**<br />Use the **`Operating`** account (or another Splits account using [Splits Connect](/getting-started/connecting-to-other-apps))

![](/images/auto-withhold-for-taxes/img-2.gif)

## Set Diversifier to receive revenue

Tap the address at the top of the Diversifier page to copy it. Then you'll want to set that as the payout address for whatever you're building (e.g. if it's a Clanker, set the recipient on your token's [clanker.world](http://clanker.world) page).

![](/images/auto-withhold-for-taxes/img-3.gif)

## Process funds

As your Diversifier accrues revenue, you'll periodically need to hit the `Swap` button as shown below when connected as the owner. That sends the funds to your **`Tax`** and **`Operating`** accounts.

![](/images/auto-withhold-for-taxes/img-4.gif)

## Funds appear in accounts

You should see USDC appear in your **`Tax`** and all other tokens in the **`Operating`** account.

![](/images/auto-withhold-for-taxes/img-5.gif)
