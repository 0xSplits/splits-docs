# Manage a Farcaster account

Farcaster is a social network built on Ethereum. In this guide, we'll walk through two ways that you can manage your Farcaster account with Splits: pre-pay for data on the network and secure your account in case of an emergency.

## Pay for storage

On Farcaster, every message published by an account (casts, reactions, profile updates) takes up some space on the network. Just like iCloud, each year you pay for the space your data uses.

To purchase storage credits, you'll need to call the `StorageRegistry` contract on Optimism, whose address is `0x00000000fcCe7f938e7aE6D3c335bD6a1a7c593D`. We'll walk through the steps below to do that in Splits.

To get started, you'll want to head to the [**Custom transaction**](https://teams.splits.org/custom-txn/) page in Splits Teams. Select an account, and paste in the contract address above. You should see the contract resolve and the `Function to call` dropdown appear on the page:

![](/images/manage-a-farcaster-account/img-1.png)

Select the `rent` function. Enter your FID and units — we'll start with `1` unit:

![](/images/manage-a-farcaster-account/img-2.png)

The current cost per unit of storage is `0.000103835485171385` (around $0.20). Multiply that value by the number of units you want to rent, and then enter it into the `Amount to pay` field.

Hit the `Review` button and you should see a summary of the transaction, like so:

![](/images/manage-a-farcaster-account/img-3.png)

Submit the transaction and you are good to go! Your account has an extra unit of storage.

## Securing your account

On Farcaster, each account is controlled by two addresses on Ethereum:

1. **Custody address:** owns the account, signs all messages to verify authenticity, and controls which apps can publish messages to the network on your behalf
2. **Recovery address:** can change the custody address if you ever lose access to its private key

When you sign up through the Farcaster app, it manages both addresses for you under the hood. But for increased security, we recommend setting your **recovery address** to a Splits account.

To do this, go to the Farcaster app's **Settings** tab and tap **Advanced** and then **Change recovery address** here:

![](/images/manage-a-farcaster-account/img-4.png)

Just set it to a Teams account of your choice and you're good to go. In the next section, we'll detail how to recover ownership of your Farcaster account if you ever lose access to it.

## Recover your account

If you ever get locked out of your Farcaster account — you lose the private key to your **custody address**, or the **recovery phrase** generated for you in the Farcaster app, you can use your **recovery address** to change ownership of your account by calling the `idRegistry` contract.

The address of the contract on Optimism is `0x00000000Fc6c5F01Fc30151999387Bb99A9f489b`.

To get started, you'll want to head to the [**Custom transaction**](https://teams.splits.org/custom-txn/) page in Splits Teams. Select an account, and paste in the contract address above. You should see the contract resolve and the `Function to call` dropdown appear on the page:

![](/images/manage-a-farcaster-account/img-5.png)

You'll want to call the `transfer` function, which will allow you to change the **custody address** for your Farcaster account to another Ethereum account. There are two restrictions:

1. you must be able to generate an EIP-712 typed signature from the new custody address
2. the new custody address must not already own an FID
