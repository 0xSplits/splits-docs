# Viewing and claiming liquidity position (LP) rewards

*Last updated 11 February 2026*

## View LP rewards

Splits automatically shows available rewards from Clanker, Uniswap V3, and Uniswap V4. Open the account receiving the rewards and look under *Positions*. You'll see all available rewards.

![](/images/viewing-and-claiming-lp-rewards/img-1.png)

### Uniswap

Rewards from Uniswap V3 and Uniswap V4 pools will appear automatically in the sidebar once you've opened a position with your Splits account.

### Clanker

WETH, USDC, and CLANKER rewards for Clanker v4 will appear automatically. If you have other tokens you are receiving rewards in (i.e. your deployed Clanker token) for v4 you will need to add the address. For Clanker v3 you will always need to add the address for your Clanker token for indexing, regardless of what token the rewards are paid out.

Select the plus button next to the *Positions* header and enter the token address. You only need to do this once and it will be saved and viewable for anyone on your team.

![](/images/viewing-and-claiming-lp-rewards/img-2.png)

![](/images/viewing-and-claiming-lp-rewards/img-3.png)

## Claim LP rewards

From the *Positions* section you can claim your rewards, just click on the balance and select *Claim* from the dropdown. It will generate a transaction for you.

![](/images/viewing-and-claiming-lp-rewards/img-4.png)

For Clanker v4, you claim one token at a time, even if you are receiving your rewards in two tokens (e.g. your native Clanker & WETH). For Clanker v3 and Uniswap, you claim for all rewards in the position (it could be one or two tokens).
