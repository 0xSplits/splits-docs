# Viewing and claiming vesting tokens

*Last updated 4 December 2025*

## View vesting streams

Splits automatically shows incoming vesting streams from **Hedgey** and **Sablier**. Open the account receiving the vesting stream, and look under *Positions*

You'll see all active streams and their claimable amounts.

![](/images/viewing-and-claiming-vesting-tokens/img-1.png)

When applicable, a [*Cliff*](https://www.investopedia.com/ask/answers/09/what-is-cliff-vesting.asp) pill will display the **unlock date** when hovered over.

![](/images/viewing-and-claiming-vesting-tokens/img-2.png)

## View details and claim vested tokens

Click on the vesting position and then *View details* to see details about your vesting stream.

![](/images/viewing-and-claiming-vesting-tokens/img-3.png)

You can claim from either the account page under *Positions* or from the plan details modal. Clicking *Claim* on either view will move tokens into your team's account.

![](/images/viewing-and-claiming-vesting-tokens/img-4.png)

<details>
<summary>**Important note:** Sablier v3 streams have a $0.90 fee required for every claim. You must have ETH in your account to successfully claim these streams.</summary>

![](/images/viewing-and-claiming-vesting-tokens/img-5.png)

</details>

Each stream includes a link to open the vesting plan on the originating protocol.

![](/images/viewing-and-claiming-vesting-tokens/img-6.png)

**Note:** You will need to connect your account to view details on Hedgey.
