# Transforming appcoins into working capital

*Last updated 11 November 2025*

Selling a token puts downward pressure on price. For appcoin builders and large holders, this creates tension. Public sales can unsettle communities, yet converting tokens into working capital (e.g. USDC) is essential.

After talking to builders, we've found a few ways that allow them (and large investors) to generate working capital from an appcoin without tanking the market.

## Scheduled sells (TWAP)

You can sell a pre-determined amount on a set frequency using the [Schedules](/getting-started/schedules) feature. You will want to create a schedule that transfers a set amount of the **output token** (e.g. USDC, or whatever token you want to buy) from one of your accounts to another of your accounts.

![](/images/transforming-appcoins-into-working-capital/img-1.png)

You'll receive an email when your transaction is created. Open the link and **select the asset you want to sell** in order to complete the transaction. This will be the same as the [just-in-time swap flow](/getting-started/swapping).

![](/images/transforming-appcoins-into-working-capital/img-2.png)

If you're in multiple teams, you'll only be able to execute the transaction when logged in to the right team (you may need to switch teams).

## Single-sided LPs

Using Uniswap, you can specify the price range for the liquidity you wish to provide. As long as the liquidity you provide is outside the current price range, your token will convert progressively into the paired token (e.g. ETH, USDC) as swaps occur within your range.

This effectively acts like a limit order that only executes if price moves into your range. For example, if your token is trading at $1 and you provide single-sided liquidity in the $1.05–$1.50 range, your position will only begin to sell once the market trades up into that band. As price continues to rise, your token is sold progressively into USDC (or the paired asset).

Choose the token pair: what you're selling and what you want back. SSLs only work in the pools where trading occurs. Choose the fee tier with deepest liquidity so your position unlocks.

If all tiers are empty, choose the standard 0.30% fee tier. It's the most common for new tokens and is most likely to attract routing and build depth over time.

![](/images/transforming-appcoins-into-working-capital/img-3.png)

Then specify a custom range. Uniswap lets you select the token you're depositing and specify a range above the current price. This is often labeled as a "one-sided upper position".

![](/images/transforming-appcoins-into-working-capital/img-4.png)

Set your range outside the current price. Otherwise, Uniswap will require liquidity in **both tokens** instead of just the one you're selling.

![](/images/transforming-appcoins-into-working-capital/img-5.png)

Monitor your SSL at [app.uniswap.org/pools](http://app.uniswap.org/pools). When your tokens have been sold, withdraw your liquidity to close your SSL.

## OTC

Over-the-counter (OTC) trades are direct, negotiated transactions between buyer and seller. These transactions happen off market, which lets large holders convert tokens into working capital without creating visible onchain sell pressure or affecting the tokens spot price. OTC sales are useful when liquidity is thin and/or discretion is important. Often these trades happen at a discount to market (reflecting the lack of liquidity at spot) and require the buyer to lock-up and/or vest their purchase.
