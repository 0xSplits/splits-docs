# Use an ENS name with Splits

## Requirements

- Mainnet should be turned on in [Settings](https://app.splits.org/settings/team/accounts/)
- You'll need enough mainnet ETH to complete the transaction (if purchasing a domain)

## I don't have a name yet…

1. **Connect your Splits account to the ENS app using WalletConnect**

  ![](/images/use-an-ens-name/img-1.gif)

  1. Open [app.ens.domains](http://app.ens.domains), hit *Connect*, select *WalletConnect*, copy the link.
  2. In a different tab/window, open your Splits account page, hit *Connect to apps*, paste the WalletConnect link copied in step 1, hit *Connect* and then *Approve*.
  3. Confirm your Splits account is connected by looking at the address in the top right of the ENS app and ensuring it matches your Splits account.

2. **Choose a name and select registration duration**

  ![](/images/use-an-ens-name/img-2.gif)

  :::note
  Make sure you leave "Use as primary name" on, so that upon registration this name will automatically be set to your connected Splits account address.
  :::

3. **Fill out the profile details (optional)**

  ![](/images/use-an-ens-name/img-3.gif)

  1. Add any additional info (avatar, URL, etc) you want.

4. **Confirm the purchase**

  ![](/images/use-an-ens-name/img-4.gif)

  1. After clicking *Open wallet* in the ENS app, switch to Splits and approve the transaction by clicking *Execute*.
  2. Once ENS's time delay is complete, click *Complete registration,* approve that additional transaction in Splits, and you're done!

## I have a name already…

:::note
If you have the ENS in an EOA, we recommend first updating the profile and pointer address *before* transferring the name to Splits. You can do it from Splits, but it will cost more and take more time if your account requires more than 1 approval.
:::

1. **Sign into ENS from the wallet that currently holds the name**

  ![](/images/use-an-ens-name/img-1.gif)

  1. Open [app.ens.domains](http://app.ens.domains), hit *Connect*, select *WalletConnect*, copy the link.
  2. In a different tab/window, open your Splits account page, hit *Connect to apps*, paste the WalletConnect link copied in step 1, hit *Connect* and then *Approve*. If you're using an EOA like Rainbow, scan the WalletConnect QR code to connect.

2. **Update the profile details and point the eth address field to the Splits account**

  ![](/images/use-an-ens-name/img-5.gif)

  1. Add any additional info (avatar, URL, etc) you want.

3. **Send the ENS name NFT to the Splits account**

  ![](/images/use-an-ens-name/img-6.gif)

  1. You can send an ENS name like any other NFT

4. **Sign into ENS with Splits and tap "set as primary name" on the account page**

  ![](/images/use-an-ens-name/img-7.gif)

  1. From the dropdown in the top-right corner of [app.ens.domains](http://app.ens.domains), hit *Disconnect* and then *Connect*, select *WalletConnect*, and copy the link.
  2. In a different tab/window, open the Splits account page that you just sent the NFT to, hit *Connect to apps*, paste the WalletConnect link copied in step 1, hit *Connect* and then *Approve*.
  3. Tap *Set as primary name* and confirm the transaction from your Splits account.

## Extend your ENS name with subnames

:::note
This section is WIP
:::

:::note
**Subnames** extend a name to point to multiple accounts (e.g. `treasury.splits.eth`, `operating.splits.eth`, `grants.splits.eth`). You keep a consistent brand across your team's accounts, without having to register a brand new name for each one.

Best of all, they work in any ENS-enabled app, just like any other ENS name would!
:::

1. **Sign into the ENS app from the wallet that controls the name you want to extend**
2. **On the account page, tap the *Subnames* header and then *Add a subname***
3. **Choose a subname, fill out the profile, and then confirm the transaction**

  :::note
  Make sure to set the ETH address field to the Splits account address you want the subname to point to!
  :::

4. **Sign out and sign in with the Splits account you want the subname to point to**
5. **On the subname account page, tap *Set as primary name***
