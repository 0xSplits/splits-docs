# Recover assets from unsupported networks

*Last updated 17 December 2025*

Recover assets sent to an unsupported network by swapping them into a supported asset directly from your account.

## Recover an asset

Open the account that received the asset on the unsupported network and navigate to *Account settings.*

![](/images/recover-assets-from-unsupported-networks/img-1.png)

Select *Asset recovery*, and you will see a list of balances this account has on unsupported networks. Select a unsupported network to view the recovery swap page.

![](/images/recover-assets-from-unsupported-networks/img-2.png)

Choose the asset you want to recover, then select *Max amount* to use the full balance.

![](/images/recover-assets-from-unsupported-networks/img-3.png)

Choose the supported network and asset you want to swap into.

![](/images/recover-assets-from-unsupported-networks/img-4.png)

Review the quoted amounts and confirm the swap details. Approve the transaction when prompted.

![](/images/recover-assets-from-unsupported-networks/img-5.png)

Sign using the required passkeys or signers for the account.

Once confirmed, the swapped asset will appear in your account balance.

![](/images/recover-assets-from-unsupported-networks/img-6.png)

## Notes

Asset recovery uses a swap to move value from unsupported networks into supported assets. Availability and pricing depend on liquidity and network conditions. If an asset does not appear, contact us at support@splits.org.
