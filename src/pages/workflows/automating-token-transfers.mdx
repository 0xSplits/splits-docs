# Automating token transfers

*Last updated 27 January 2026*

*If you want to automate more than token transfers, see [Automating custom transactions](/workflows/automating-custom-transactions).*

You can programmatically transfer tokens held in a Splits account using ERC20 approvals. This allows you to build apps that automatically transfer tokens (e.g. appcoins) when certain actions are taken, while still managing those tokens in Splits.

![](/images/automating-token-transfers/img-1.png)

## Create a dedicated sub account to hold tokens

Because you will be granting a third party account (i.e. the spender) approval to transfer tokens from a Splits account, we suggest creating a dedicated sub account specifically for this purpose.

![](/images/automating-token-transfers/img-2.png)

For our demo, we'll call this account "App Distributions", since it is where funds will be distributed from via our app.

## Approve the spender on the ERC20 contract

Before approving the ERC20, it is helpful to transfer some of that token to the "App Distributions" account you just created. For our demo, we're going to use USDC.

![](/images/automating-token-transfers/img-3.png)

Next, we will give a third party account (i.e. the spender) the ability to transfer USDC from the "App Distributions" account.

We will do this by writing to the USDC contract directly using [Custom transactions](/getting-started/custom-transactions). Copy the token's contract address to your clipboard, and then click *Custom txn* in the top right.

![](/images/automating-token-transfers/img-4.png)

Then paste the token's contract address and select `approve` as the *Function to call*.

![](/images/automating-token-transfers/img-5.png)

- **Spender** is where you'll paste the address of the account for which you have a private key and can initiate transactions from your app.
- **Value** is the quantity of tokens (e.g. USDC) you wish to approve the spender to transfer. For unlimited approvals (i.e. the entire balance of your USDC held in your Splits sub account), enter: `115792089237316195423570985008687907853269984665640564039457584007913129639935`. [See this list for more constants](https://constants.swiss-knife.xyz/).

Hit review to see a preview of the transaction, and ensure everything looks correct. Then submit and sign the transaction.

![](/images/automating-token-transfers/img-6.png)

Once the transaction is confirmed, you will see it in your transactions feed.

![](/images/automating-token-transfers/img-7.png)

You have now granted the **spender approval to transfer your tokens**. Now, you can use the private key of the spender to trigger the transfers from your own app. And anytime a transfer is made, you will see it show up in Splits.

## Example: Transferring via approved spender

In the above walkthrough, we granted another Splits account the ability to transfer from the "App Distributions" account. So to confirm this works as expected, we can initiate a transfer.

We'll again use [Custom transactions](/getting-started/custom-transactions) to call the `transferFrom` function on the USDC contract.

![](/images/automating-token-transfers/img-8.png)

In the above screenshot, 1 USDC will be transferred by the "Spender" account from the "App Distributions" account to the "Receiver" account. Once that transaction confirm, you will see it show up in the transactions feed.

![](/images/automating-token-transfers/img-9.png)
