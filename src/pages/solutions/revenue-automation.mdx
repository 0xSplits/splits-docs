# Revenue automation [Revenue lands in an account per stream, converts on receipt, and funds scheduled payments, all from self-custodied accounts]

**Revenue automation** on Splits is money movement: an [automation account](/accounts#automation-accounts) per revenue stream that swaps, splits, forwards, or offramps whatever lands at its address, and [schedules](/transactions/schedules) that draft the recurring payments out. Every automation runs from an account that the team's own signers control and that the Treasury owns.

## How it works

- **One automation account per revenue stream.** Each [automation account](/accounts#automation-accounts) is a deposit address with a policy; runs start automatically once a balance reaches the trigger amount, and each token is swapped, bridged, forwarded, or offramped to the policy's destinations. Typical policies sweep everything to USDC, withhold a share for taxes, or split income across accounts, partners, and the bank.
- **Runs need no approval; scheduled payments do.** An automation's signer set is the Treasury's passkeys plus a Splits server key at 1-of-n, so runs execute on their own. A [schedule](/transactions/schedules) produces a draft proposal per occurrence that the account's signers approve at its [threshold](/accounts/thresholds).
- **Swaps and bridges carry no Splits fee.** Each [swap](/transactions/swaps) quote is routed across several providers, including cross-network routes.
- **Invoices reconcile onchain.** An invoice paid through its page is marked paid once Splits verifies the transaction; bank payments are [marked manually](/invoicing/tracking). An invoice can be [issued](/invoicing#issuing) to receive directly into an automation's deposit address.
- **Idle stables earn without lockup.** Automated [Earn](/accounts/earn) deposits an account's idle balance per account; positions can be withdrawn at any time and spent directly.
- **The books follow.** Every run and payment appears on the [Accounting](/accounting) page, filterable and exportable, and an accountant joins as a [read-only member](/teams/roles#read-only-members).

## Setting up a revenue stream

Prerequisites: a [team](/teams) with the [Owner](/teams/roles) role, and [banking](/banking) set up if a destination is a bank account.

::::steps

### Create the automation account

Create one [automation account](/accounts#automation-accounts) per stream and set its policy: the output token, the accounts and partners that each share goes to, and any bank destination.

### Point revenue at it

Give the account's address to whatever pays the stream: a contract, a platform payout, or an [invoice](/invoicing#issuing) whose receiving account is the automation.

### Schedule the payments out

Add a [schedule](/transactions/schedules) from the account that pays payroll and vendors. [Vendors paid in fiat](/banking/paying-vendors) need a bank account added first.

### Turn on Automated Earn

Enable [Automated Earn](/accounts/earn#automated-earn) on the accounts that hold working capital between payment runs.

### Hand over the books

Invite the accountant as a [read-only member](/teams/roles#read-only-members) and export from [Accounting](/accounting).

::::

## Limits

- **Automations and schedules move money.** [Schedules](/transactions/schedules) handle transfers only. Automating a permissioned contract call takes a [module](/accounts/modules) with a key that you run yourself.
- Automation accounts are operated by Splits. For a self-hosted equivalent, the open-source [Swapper](https://splits.org/protocol/docs/core/swapper) contract converts incoming tokens to one output token; the trade-offs are in [Automating long-tail token conversion](https://splits.org/blog/automating-long-tail-token-conversion/).

## Programmatic access

Via the [Splits CLI / MCP](/introduction/agents):

- `splits automations list`: the team's automations (**Read** scope)
- `splits transactions list`: every run and payment, filterable by account, direction, amount, and date (**Read** scope)

Creating automations, schedules, and invoices is web-only today.
