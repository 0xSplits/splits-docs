# Revenue automation [Revenue lands in an account per stream, converts on receipt, and funds scheduled payments, all from self-custodied accounts]

**Revenue automation** on Splits is money movement. An [automation account](/accounts#automation-accounts) per revenue stream swaps, splits, forwards, or offramps whatever arrives at its address. [Schedules](/transactions/schedules) draft the recurring outgoing payments. Every automation runs from an account that the team's own signers control and that the Treasury owns.

## How it works

- **One automation account per revenue stream.** Each automation account is a deposit address with a policy. A run starts automatically once a balance reaches the trigger amount. The run swaps, bridges, forwards, or offramps each token to the policy's destinations. Typical policies sweep everything to USDC, withhold a share for taxes, or split income across accounts, partners, and the bank.
- **Runs need no approval. Scheduled payments need approval.** An automation's signer set is the Treasury's passkeys plus a Splits server key at 1-of-n, so runs execute on their own. A schedule produces one draft proposal per occurrence. The account's signers approve each proposal at the account's [threshold](/accounts/thresholds).
- **Swaps and bridges carry no Splits fee.** Splits routes each [swap](/transactions/swaps) quote across several providers, including cross-network routes.
- **Invoices reconcile onchain.** When a payer pays an invoice through its page, Splits marks the invoice paid after it verifies the transaction. You mark bank payments manually: see [Tracking payments](/invoicing/tracking). You can [issue](/invoicing#issuing) an invoice that receives directly into an automation's deposit address.
- **Idle stables earn without lockup.** Automated [Earn](/accounts/earn) deposits an account's idle balance. You enable it per account. You can withdraw positions at any time and spend them directly.
- **The books follow.** Every run and payment appears on the [Accounting](/accounting) page, where you can filter and export them. An accountant joins as a [read-only member](/teams/roles#read-only-members).

## Setting up a revenue stream

Prerequisites: a [team](/teams) with the [Owner](/teams/roles) role, and [banking](/banking) set up if a destination is a bank account.

::::steps

### Create the automation account

Create one automation account per stream. Then set its policy:

- the output token
- the accounts and partners that receive each share
- any bank destination

### Send revenue to the account

Give the account's address to whatever pays the stream. The payer can be a contract, a platform payout, or an invoice whose receiving account is the automation.

### Schedule the outgoing payments

Add a schedule from the account that pays payroll and vendors. For a vendor paid in fiat, add the vendor's bank account first: see [Paying vendors](/banking/paying-vendors).

### Enable Automated Earn

Enable [Automated Earn](/accounts/earn#automated-earn) on the accounts that hold working capital between payment runs.

### Invite the accountant

Invite the accountant as a [read-only member](/teams/roles#read-only-members). Export the books from Accounting.

::::

## Limits

- **Automations and schedules move money.** Schedules handle transfers only. Automating a permissioned contract call needs a [module](/accounts/modules) with a key that you run yourself.
- Splits operates automation accounts. For a self-hosted equivalent, the open-source [Swapper](https://splits.org/protocol/docs/core/swapper) contract converts incoming tokens to one output token. The trade-offs are in [Automating long-tail token conversion](https://splits.org/blog/automating-long-tail-token-conversion/).

## Programmatic access

Via the [Splits CLI / MCP](/introduction/agents):

- `splits automations list`: the team's automations (**Read** scope)
- `splits transactions list`: every run and payment, filterable by account, direction, amount, and date (**Read** scope)

Creating automations, schedules, and invoices is web-only today.
