# Crypto offramp [Send USDC from a Splits account to a bank account by ACH, wire, or SEPA]

A **crypto offramp** on Splits is a crypto to bank account transfer. Splits converts USDC from a [workspace](/workspaces) account to dollars or euros. Splits then sends the money to your bank account by ACH or wire in the US, or by SEPA in the EU.

## How do I send USDC to my bank account?

Verify the workspace, add a bank account, then send USDC to that account from *Send*.

Prerequisites: the [Owner](/workspaces/roles) role in the workspace.

::::steps

### Verify the workspace

Go to [Settings > Banks](https://app.splits.org/settings/team/banks/). Select *KYB* for a business or *KYC* for an individual. Then complete [entity verification](/banking#getting-verified).

### Add a bank account

Connect a US [bank account](/banking/offramping) with Plaid, or enter its routing and account numbers. For an EU account, enter its IBAN.

### Open Send

Open *Send*. Choose the bank account as the recipient.

### Enter the amount

Enter the amount. To set the amount that the bank receives, use [*Recipient gets*](/banking/offramping#exact-bank-amount). The review step shows the amount sent, the fee, and the amount that the bank receives.

### Approve the transaction

The offramp is a [transaction](/transactions) proposal. It executes when signers meet the account's [threshold](/accounts/thresholds).

::::

## Which bank accounts can receive USDC?

A business or personal bank account can receive USDC, if the account is in the name of the workspace's verified entity. The workspace verifies as a business with KYB or as an individual with KYC.

US accounts receive by ACH or wire. EU accounts receive by SEPA.

To pay someone else's bank account, see [Paying vendors](/banking/paying-vendors).

## Can I send USDC to my Mercury account?

Yes. Mercury business accounts accept ACH and wire transfers, and Splits sends USDC to Mercury that way.

Mercury accounts hold only fiat. Splits converts the USDC to dollars first.

We use this ourselves: Splits offramps to its own Mercury account.

Splits is not affiliated with Mercury.

## How long does it take, and what does it cost?

In the US, Same Day ACH settles in batches throughout the day. For IBAN banks that support SEPA Instant, settlement takes under 30 seconds.

In Eastern Time, ACH settlement occurs at 1:00 pm, 5:00 pm, and 6:00 pm. The cutoffs are 9:15 am, 1:30 pm, and 3:30 pm. An offramp sent after 3:30 pm ET arrives the next business day. See [Settlement](/banking/offramping#settlement).

The [fee](/banking) is 0.25%, deducted from the transaction amount. The minimum amount that the bank can receive is $10 or €10.

With *Recipient gets*, the app increases the amount sent to cover the fee. For example, to receive $1,995.00, the account sends 2,000.00 USDC. The $5.00 difference covers the fee.

## Where is it available?

Splits supports US- and EU-based individuals and businesses. The exclusions are [countries that Bridge does not support](https://apidocs.bridge.xyz/platform/customers/compliance/supported-countries-list#supported-countries-list), and New York and Alaska in the US ([Bridge's US details](https://apidocs.bridge.xyz/get-started/introduction/what-we-support/geo#united-states-specific-details)).

If you live elsewhere, you can still offramp. Your entity must be legally registered in the US, and you need a US or EU bank account. See [Jurisdictions](/banking#jurisdictions).

## Programmatic access

Via the [Splits CLI / MCP](/introduction/agents):

- `splits offramp external-accounts`: the workspace's bank accounts (**Owner** scope)
- `splits transactions create offramp --account 0x... --external-account-id <id> --amount 100`: propose an offramp of the amount that the bank receives (**Write** and **Owner** scopes)

Adding bank accounts is web-only today.
