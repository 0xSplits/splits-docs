# Business banking [Self-custodied multi-sig accounts with bank transfers, vendor payments, invoicing, and accounting]

**Business banking** on Splits is a team's own [accounts](/accounts), each a multi-sig smart account that the team's signing keys control, plus opt-in [banking](/banking) that moves between those accounts and bank accounts once the team's entity is verified.

## Splits versus Coinbase Business and Fireblocks

[Coinbase Business](https://www.coinbase.com/business), like a crypto-friendly bank, holds the funds for you. [Fireblocks](https://www.fireblocks.com/) splits MPC key shares with the customer. A Splits account is a contract that the team's own keys control:

| Property | Coinbase Business | Fireblocks | Splits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Custody | Coinbase | Key shares split between the customer and Fireblocks; a transaction needs the customer's signature | The team's own signing keys |
| Approvals | Set in Coinbase, offchain | Policy Engine rules: block, approve, or require more signers | M-of-N signers per account, onchain |
| To open | A legal entity in the US or Singapore | Self-serve signup on the Essentials plan | An email; verify a business or an individual to add bank transfers |
| Pricing | Fees per payment and per conversion ([Coinbase Business](https://www.coinbase.com/business)) | Subscription from $999 per month ([pricing](https://www.fireblocks.com/pricing)) | A per-transfer fee on bank legs ([pricing](https://splits.org/pricing/)) |

## How it works

- **Accounts are self-custodied and multi-sig by default.** Every account has an M-of-N [threshold](/accounts/thresholds) set by the team, the Treasury's at [setup](/teams#the-setup-steps); recovery signers can reset any account ([recovery](/teams/recovery)).
- **Fiat moves by bank transfer.** After entity verification, money comes in by ACH, wire, or SEPA and goes out to the team's own bank accounts; the fee and jurisdictions are on [Banking](/banking).
- **Vendors and contractors are paid in fiat from stables.** A vendor's bank account gets a deposit address, and paying it is a stablecoin send approved at the account's threshold ([paying vendors](/banking/paying-vendors)). Tax forms are collected through [Compliance](/contacts/compliance).
- **Payroll is a schedule.** Each occurrence is a draft that the signers approve ([schedules](/transactions/schedules)).
- **Invoices reconcile automatically** when paid through the invoice page, in crypto or by bank transfer ([invoicing](/invoicing)).
- **The books export.** Every transaction across every account is filterable and exportable to CSV, with tax lots and realized gains ([accounting](/accounting)); an accountant joins as a [read-only member](/teams/roles#read-only-members).
- **Core tools are free.** Accounts, accounting, invoicing, payments, and swaps are free; bank transfers and fiat payments are charged per transfer ([pricing](https://splits.org/pricing/)).

## Opening an account

Prerequisites: a [passkey](/members/keys) saved in a password manager.

::::steps

### Create a team

[Team setup](/teams#the-setup-steps) takes a name, your passkey, the Treasury's threshold, and recovery signers.

### Add accounts per purpose

Create an [operating account](/accounts#operating-accounts) per revenue source, program, or partner, each its own sub-ledger.

### Verify the entity for fiat

Complete [entity verification](/banking#getting-verified) as a business, or as an individual before incorporating ([no entity needed](/introduction/personal-usage#no-entity-needed)).

### Connect banks and vendors

Add the team's bank accounts for [onramps](/banking/onramping) and [offramps](/banking/offramping), and vendors' accounts for [fiat payments](/banking/paying-vendors).

### Run payroll and hand over the books

Set up [schedules](/transactions/schedules) for payroll, and invite the accountant as a [read-only member](/teams/roles#read-only-members).

::::

## Limits

- Fiat legs are available in the [jurisdictions](/banking#jurisdictions) Bridge supports, and each team verifies one entity.
- Bank settlement follows ACH and SEPA windows ([settlement](/banking/offramping#settlement)).
- Cards come through the [Rain](/integrations/rain) integration, which controls issuance, limits, and fees.

## Programmatic access

Via the [Splits CLI / MCP](/introduction/agents):

- `splits accounts list`: all accounts in the team (**Read** scope)
- `splits transactions list`: the books, filterable by account, direction, amount, date, and memo (**Read** scope)

Banking (onramps, offramps, vendor payments) and invoicing are web-only today.
