# Passkey wallet [A self-custodied ERC-4337 smart account with passkey and EOA signers]

A **Splits account** is an ERC-4337 smart-contract multisig whose signers are passkeys or EOAs on one M-of-N threshold ([wallet architecture](/accounts#wallet-architecture)). There is no seed phrase: a passkey is saved in a password manager and unlocked with biometrics or a PIN ([signing keys](/members/keys)), and a separate set of recovery signers can reset any account's signers onchain ([recovery](/teams/recovery)). The contracts are open source and audited ([security](/resources/security)).

## Splits versus MetaMask and Rainbow

[MetaMask](https://support.metamask.io/start/user-guide-secret-recovery-phrase-password-and-private-keys/) and [Rainbow](https://rainbow.me/support/app/the-importance-of-backups) are self-custodied wallets built on one secret phrase. A Splits account is a self-custodied contract with a signer set:

| Property | MetaMask and Rainbow | Splits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How you sign | A password, then a key derived from the phrase | A passkey, with biometrics or a PIN |
| Approvals per transaction | One key | A threshold you set, 1-of-2 up to M-of-N |
| Recovery | Your own backup of the phrase | Recovery signers reset the account's signers onchain |
| Account type | EOA | ERC-4337 smart account, with sponsored gas and batched transactions |
| Working with others | Share the phrase, or a separate wallet each | Invite members and add them as signers |
| Agents | The agent needs the phrase itself | The agent gets its own key on a dedicated account |
| Fiat | Buy and sell through third-party providers | Bank transfers to and from your own bank account, after [verification](/banking) |
| Books | The wallet's activity list | The [Accounting](/accounting) page, with CSV export |
| Dapps | Built in | [Splits Connect](/introduction/extension), the browser extension |

## How it works

- **Passkeys and EOAs are equal signers.** A signer is any [signing key](/members/keys) added to an account's signer set, counted against the [threshold](/accounts/thresholds); the private key never leaves the member ([signers](/accounts/signers)).
- **Multi-sig by default.** Every account has its own threshold. A solo builder runs 1-of-2 or 2-of-3 across their own devices and keys ([multisigs of one](/introduction/personal-usage#multisigs-of-one)).
- **Recovery is contract-enforced.** Recovery signers are EOAs of your choosing, such as hardware wallets or a trusted person's wallet, that control the Root account; because the Root owns every other account, they can reset any account's signers ([recovery](/teams/recovery)).
- **An account per project.** Each account is a sub-ledger with its own signers and threshold, owned by the Treasury ([operating accounts](/accounts#operating-accounts), [isolated accounts](/introduction/personal-usage#isolated-accounts)).
- **Start solo, add collaborators later.** Membership and signing are separate: inviting a [member](/members) grants visibility and a role, and adding them as a signer is a separate onchain action ([signers vs membership](/accounts/signers#signers-vs-membership)).
- **Fiat as an individual.** Verify as an individual to move between the accounts and a personal bank account ([no entity needed](/introduction/personal-usage#no-entity-needed)).
- **Agents can be signers too.** An agent's own EOA joins an account's signer set like any other key ([AI agents](/solutions/ai-agents)).

## Creating your wallet

Prerequisites: a [password manager](/members/keys#password-managers) that stores passkeys.

::::steps

### Sign up with a passkey

[Sign up](/members#signing-up) and add a passkey; it becomes your signer on the Treasury.

### Create a team of one

Run [team setup](/teams#the-setup-steps), skipping the Owners and Treasury steps, and add recovery signers: at least one EOA, on a hardware wallet if you have one.

### Make it a multisig of one

Add a second passkey or an EOA to the Treasury and raise its threshold ([changing signers and thresholds](/accounts/editing#changing-signers-and-thresholds)).

### Add an account per project

Create an [operating account](/accounts#operating-accounts) for each project. Keep the bulk in the Treasury and give day-to-day accounts a lower threshold.

### Verify recovery periodically

[Verify each recovery signer](/teams/recovery#verifying-recovery-signers) without moving funds; each shows when it was last verified.

::::

## Limits

- Passkeys are bound to splits.org: if the site were offline, they could not sign, which is why recovery signers are ordinary Ethereum keys ([why recovery exists](/teams/recovery#why-recovery-exists)).
- Accounts exist on the [supported networks](/introduction/networks-and-assets).

## Programmatic access

Via the [Splits CLI / MCP](/introduction/agents):

- `splits accounts signers <address>`: an account's signers and threshold (**Read** scope)
- `splits auth register-signer <address>`: register an EOA so it can be added as a signer

Passkeys are added in the app only.
