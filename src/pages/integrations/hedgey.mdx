# Hedgey [View and claim vesting and lockup tokens]

**[Hedgey](https://hedgey.finance)** provides token vesting and lockup plans. Plans that pay a Splits account appear as [positions](/integrations#positions).

The position shows the amount available to claim. If the plan has a cliff, it also shows the first release date.

A claim transfers available tokens into the account. Each position links to its Hedgey plan.

For full plan information:

1. Open the position's Hedgey link.
2. Connect the account through [WalletConnect](/integrations/walletconnect) or the [browser extension](/introduction/extension).
