# Hedgey [View and claim Hedgey vesting and lockup streams]

[Hedgey](https://hedgey.finance) issues onchain token vesting and lockup plans. Plans paying a Splits account appear as [positions](/integrations#positions) on the account's page, showing the claimable amount and, when the plan has a cliff, its unlock date.

Claiming moves the vested tokens into the account. Each position links to the plan on Hedgey; viewing full details there requires connecting the account to Hedgey's site (via [WalletConnect](/integrations/walletconnect) or the [extension](/introduction/extension)).
