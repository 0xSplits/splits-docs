# Clanker [View and claim Clanker token rewards]

[Clanker](https://clanker.world) pays LP rewards to token deployers. Rewards paying a Splits account appear as [positions](/integrations#positions) on the account's page.

Which rewards index automatically depends on the Clanker version:

- **Clanker v4** — rewards paid in WETH, USDC, and CLANKER appear automatically; rewards in any other token (e.g. your own deployed token) require adding that token's address.
- **Clanker v3** — always requires adding your Clanker token's address, regardless of the reward token.

Add a token from the plus button next to the *Positions* header; it's saved once for the whole team.

Claiming moves the rewards into the account: per token for v4, and for the whole position (one or two tokens) for v3. To route Clanker revenue automatically, see [automation accounts](/accounts#automation-accounts).
