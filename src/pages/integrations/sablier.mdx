# Sablier [View and claim Sablier vesting streams]

[Sablier](https://sablier.com) streams token vesting onchain. Sablier v2 and v3 streams that pay a Splits account appear as [positions](/integrations#positions) on the account page. Each position shows the claimable amount. If the stream has a cliff, the position also shows its unlock date.

Claiming moves the vested tokens into the account. **Sablier v3 charges a fee on every claim**, shown in the claim dialog. The account must hold enough of the fee token (ETH) to claim. Each position links to the stream on Sablier.
