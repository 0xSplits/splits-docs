# Sablier [View and claim Sablier vesting streams]

[Sablier](https://sablier.com) streams token vesting onchain. Sablier v2 and v3 streams paying a Splits account appear as [positions](/integrations#positions) on the account's page, showing the claimable amount and, when the stream has a cliff, its unlock date.

Claiming moves the vested tokens into the account. **Sablier v3 charges a fee on every claim**, shown in the claim dialog; the account must hold enough of the fee token (ETH) to claim. Each position links to the stream on Sablier.
