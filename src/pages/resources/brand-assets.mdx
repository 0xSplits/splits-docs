# Brand assets [Download Splits logos, wordmarks, and product images]

**Brand assets** are the logos, wordmarks, and product images for Splits. The [brand repository](https://github.com/0xSplits/brand) contains the files.

| Asset | Files | Formats |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Logos](https://github.com/0xSplits/brand/tree/main/logos) | `splits`, `splits_compressed`, `splits_wordmark` | SVG, PNG |
| [Product images](https://github.com/0xSplits/brand/tree/main/screenshots) | App screenshots | Image files |

Logos have light and dark versions.
