# Private transfers [Send without an onchain link between sender and recipient]

A private transfer is a [send](/transactions/sends) that settles through [NEAR Intents](https://near-intents.org) instead of moving tokens directly from your account to the recipient. Every private transfer runs as a [Confidential Intent](https://www.near.org/blog/confidential-intents): settlement executes on NEAR's private shard, inside trusted execution environments (TEEs) that hide amounts and routing even from the shard's validators, and the recipient is paid out in a separate transaction. Nothing on any public chain connects the two, so the payment the recipient receives can't be traced back to the sending account.

## What is and isn't hidden

Private refers to the onchain link between sender and recipient, nothing more:

- The deposit leaving your account is a normal onchain transaction, visible to anyone watching the account. It goes to a NEAR Intents deposit address, not to the recipient.
- The payout reaching the recipient is a normal onchain transaction; the amount is not hidden.
- The two are unlinked: the settlement between them happens on NEAR's private shard, so nothing on any public chain ties the recipient's payout to the sending account.

:::note
Members of your team can still view the recipient and amount from the transaction feed, like any other [transaction](/transactions).
:::

## How it works

A private transfer never executes as a direct transfer, even when the account already holds the token being sent and the recipient is on the same network. Every private transfer routes through the provider:

1. The app fetches a NEAR Intents quote for the exact amount the recipient should receive.
2. Once signatures meet the account's [threshold](/accounts/thresholds), the account deposits the payment token to a NEAR Intents deposit address.
3. NEAR Intents market makers fill the transfer as a Confidential Intent. Settlement executes on NEAR's private shard, connected to the public chains by a TEE-based bridge, and the recipient is paid from the market maker's own liquidity in a separate transaction.
4. Splits verifies settlement with the provider and updates the transaction once the recipient's payout lands or fails.

A private transfer either completes in full or the deposit is refunded to the sending account: failed fills are refunded, and a deposit that lands after the quote's one-hour deadline is refunded without executing.

## Sending privately

On the *Send* page, switch to the **Private** tab. The form works like a single-recipient transfer, with a few differences:

- Only [supported tokens](#supported-tokens) are offered, for both the token the recipient receives and the payment source.
- A payment source is always used, even when sending a token the account holds: it can be the sent token itself, another supported token, or a supported token on a different network.

We do not yet support multi-recipient private transfers, but plan to in the future.

## Fees

Splits charges no fees on private transfers. The amount you enter is what the recipient receives; the quote determines how much of the payment token the account deposits, and the difference between the two is the provider's pricing.
