# Swaps [Trade and bridge tokens with no fees from Splits]

*Updated 5 September 2025*

Swaps are integrated into the product in a number of ways. Regardless of which swap approach you use, they all follow the same principles:

- We charge no fees on swaps
- To ensure you get competitive prices, we use a number of providers under the hood: 0x, KyberSwap, ParaSwap
- Bridging is a contained within swaps (i.e. we consider moving between ETH on Base and ETH on Optimism a swap)

## Just in time swaps

Our goal with Splits is to save you time by speeding up your operations. One way we do this by allowing you to **send a token you don't currently have**. As long as you have the money in your account to cover the send, you can send any token regardless of whether you have it. We do this by combining a swap and send into a single transaction.

Click the *Send* button from either the Dashboard page of the account page, specify the Recipient, and then specify the **token the recipient receives**. Once you enter an amount, you will see the tokens you do have that you can sell to cover the send.

## Dedicated swap flows

If you have a token you're looking to buy or sell, you can do so from the Dashboard or Account page. This should feel familiar to other "wallets".

### Swapping from the Dashboard

On the Dashboard page, click the *Swap* button at the top. From there, you can select the account from which you wish to swap, and then select the tokens you're swapping between.

![](/images/swapping/img-2.png)

You can paste in the address of any ERC20 token to find a token that isn't already listed. Ensure you have the right network selected when doing this. In the image below, I pasted in the [VV token address on Base](https://basescan.org/address/0xd2969cc475A49e73182Ae1c517AdD57dB0F1c2AC).

![](/images/swapping/img-3.png)

### Swapping from the Account page

On the account page, in the Balances section on the right side click on a token you hold and select *Swap*. From here the flow will be the same as above, with the account pre-selected. In the image below, I am swapping to/from the Operations account, because I am viewing that account's page.

![](/images/swapping/img-4.png)

### Expiring quotes

Swapping from multisig accounts can be challenging given how quickly quotes expire. This is a known issue we are addressing. In the meantime, a few ways to circumvent this today:

1. Setup a 1-of-n sub account for swapping and transfer funds in from the higher threshold accounts.
2. If you're always always swapping to the same output token, setup an [Automation](/accounts#automation-accounts) (e.g. 100% to EURC) and just transfer funds in.
3. Use the [browser extension](/introduction/extension) to connect to Cowswap, which is more forgiving with the expiration timing.
