# What jurisdictions are supported for on/offramps?

*Last updates 26 August 2025*

*See [On/offramping](/banking) for more info on this feature.*

We support **US- and EU-based on and offramps** (individuals or businesses), with the exceptions of [countries not supported by Bridge](https://apidocs.bridge.xyz/platform/customers/compliance/supported-countries-list#supported-countries-list). We also do not support individuals or businesses based in New York or Alaska. See Bridge docs on [US-specific details](https://apidocs.bridge.xyz/get-started/introduction/what-we-support/geo#united-states-specific-details).

If you would like to request support for your jurisdiction, reach out to support@splits.org.

# Living abroad

If you live outside the US or EU (or in a non-supported country), you can still on/offramp as long as:

- Your entity is legally registered in the US, and
- You have a US- or EU-based bank account

# KYB requirements

For a full breakdown of what info is required for KYB, see [Bridge's Business Onboarding docs](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1UjwaXWHNEs3PTaj2R2l_CLxGApj8Uc8NHlL_WVd6TFY/edit?usp=sharing).

# Using Wise.com

Once you've completed KYC/B, you can use the *Add bank account* flow to connect your [Wise](http://wise.com/) account. This *should* work just like any other bank account, and potentially get around some of the jurisdiction limitations within Bridge today.
