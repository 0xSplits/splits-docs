# Can I refer my friends?

Yes, please do! We would love to meet anyone you believe would make a good fit for Splits.

Let us know you have folks to refer and **we'll send you a referral link** so your referrals are attributed to you.

You can also introduce us via email (`abram@splits.org`) or whatever channel is easiest for you.
