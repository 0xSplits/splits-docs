# Can I change Recovery signers?

Yes, but because account addresses are derived from your recovery configuration, changing recovery signers changes your account addresses. The practical path today is creating a new team.

See [Recovery](/accounts/recovery#changing-recovery-signers) for the full explanation and migration steps.
