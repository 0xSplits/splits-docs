# Why isn't my passkey opening?

We've found 1Password and Bitwarden to be somewhat finicky, especially with newer browsers (e.g. Arc/Dia). Unfortunately this isn't part of the stack we have much control over, but here are some tips on how to debug.

First, in Splits, go to Settings \> Passkeys, click the ellipses item next to the passkey name and click *Verify access.* This will prompt that specific passkey to open, allowing you to more easily troubleshoot (vs having to do this while signing transactions).

![](/images/passkey-not-opening/img-1.png)

Once you're there and can isolate the passkey in question:

- Try toggling on/off the 1Password/Bitwarden extensions
- Try a doing a hard refresh of your browser (`cmd` + `shift` + `r`)
- "WebAuthn" is the standard passkeys use, so searching that + your browser may help debug

If nothing is working and you need to recover your Accounts, please refer to [Recovering your accounts](/accounts/recovery).

# Our preferred passkeys

We've found **iCloud Keychain** and **Google Password Manager** to be the most reliable for day-to-day operations. 1Password also works well, though we find the UX slightly worse.

We use Yubikeys for higher-asset accounts (`>$50k` TVL).

You can read more about our setup in [How we secure our assets](/outlook/how-we-secure-our-assets).
