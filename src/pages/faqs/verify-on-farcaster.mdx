# How can I verify my Splits account on Farcaster?

Farcaster requires a valid [1271](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-1271) signature on Ethereum mainnet to [verify a Splits account](https://farcaster.xyz/~/settings/verified-addresses). Unfortunately, they do not support [6492](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-6492) (signatures from not-yet-deployed contracts). Therefore, to successfully prove ownership of one of your Splits accounts to Farcaster, you must:

1. Enable mainnet
2. Ensure the account is deployed. This happens on first transaction and can be verified via Etherscan's `contract` tab. If the tab isn't present, the contract hasn't been deployed yet to that network.
