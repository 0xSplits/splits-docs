# What transaction fees are sponsored?

*Last update 10 September 2025*

We sponsor some transaction fees so you can use Splits without worrying about managing gas across your active networks and Accounts. Each team gets a monthly gas stipend based on its [Earn](/accounts/earn) balance:

- **Public (\$0 Earn):** one-time \$5 stipend (does not refresh)
- **Hobbyist (\$1k Earn):** \$5 per month
- **Prosumer (\$10k Earn):** \$10 per month
- **Business (\$100k Earn):** \$100 per month

This stipend is applied at the **team level** and is shared across all your accounts. Once your monthly allowance is used, transactions will need to be paid using the network's native token (e.g. ETH). You'll see a message when submitting the transaction if you don't have enough of the network's native token to complete the transaction.

For now, you cannot view your remaining stipend. We will ship this soon.

We reserve the right to change our transaction sponsorship policies at any time without notice.
