# Experiments [Prototype products Splits ships outside the app]

An **experiment** is a product Splits ships as a standalone site with its own contracts, separate from [the app](https://app.splits.org). Experiments run **without teams, members, accounts, or roles**: an external [wallet](/members/keys#eoas) connects directly, and authorization is by wallet address.

Experiments are prototypes. Their contracts are **not audited beyond a first review**, and their flows have been exercised only with small amounts. Use them with caution.

| Experiment | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [PACT](/experiments/pact) | Raise a small round by selling a slice of a project's cap table for USDC |
