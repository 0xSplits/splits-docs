# PACT [Raise a small round by selling a slice of a project's cap table for USDC on Base]

**PACT** (Purchase Agreement for Community Tokens) is an [experiment](/experiments) at [pact.splits.org](https://pact.splits.org) for raising a small round from your community.

A project creates a cap table of 1,000 units, where one unit is 0.1% ownership, keeps the founders' units, and puts the rest up for sale. Buyers pay USDC on Base. The price rises a fixed amount with each unit sold, so early buyers pay less. If the raise hasn't reached its minimum by the close date, every buyer gets their USDC back.

The cap table is an onchain token, a [liquid split](https://splits.org/protocol/docs/templates/liquid): revenue sent to it can be paid out to holders in proportion to their units.

Units are **not equity**: they confer no legal, voting, or dividend rights in the project. Any benefits to holders are at the project's discretion. The minimum is a coordination signal; once a raise succeeds, the contracts **do not protect buyers from a dishonest project**.

:::note
PACT is a prototype; see [Experiments](/experiments) for what that means.
:::

To start a raise, go to [pact.splits.org/create](https://pact.splits.org/create) and connect a wallet on Base. There is no sign-in; the wallet that creates the raise manages it. For the exact pricing, allocation, and lifecycle rules, see the repo: [architecture](https://github.com/0xSplits/pact/blob/main/docs/architecture.md) and [contract specification](https://github.com/0xSplits/pact/blob/main/contracts/docs/contracts.md).

## Programmatic access

PACT is web-only today.
