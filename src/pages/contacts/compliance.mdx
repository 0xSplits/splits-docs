# Compliance [Collect payee identity information and tax forms]

**Compliance** collects identity information and tax forms for payees. A payee is a contractor or vendor that the team pays.

:::note
This feature is in beta.
:::

Email support to enable it for your team.

## How it works

An [Owner](/teams/roles) invites the payee by email. The payee completes identity verification through [Persona](https://withpersona.com/). The verification provider stores the tax information.

Splits generates a tax form from the verified information:

| Payee classification | Form |
| --- | --- |
| US person | W-9 |
| Foreign individual | W-8BEN |
| Foreign entity | W-8BEN-E |

After verification, Splits records payments to that payee as compliant payments. The payee also appears in the [send](/transactions/sends) recipient list.

## Year-end filings

Splits can generate a 1099 CSV for a filing year. It includes verified payee information and total USD payments for each payee.

Email support to request this export.
