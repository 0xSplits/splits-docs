# How we got here

## A brief history

- Mid 2021 - Will and Abram earned a few thousand dollars for being selected as a finalist during an ETH Global hackathon. (It was at this hackathon that the original Split contract was built!). Instead of splitting it (!), the funds were sent to a Metamask wallet that Abram created and shared the seed phrase of with Will.
- Late 2021 - We decided to "take Splits more seriously", so we purchased an ENS. We needed to secure the ENS, so we set up a Gnosis Safe on mainnet. This was a 2-of-3, where Abram, Will, and a close friend (not an employee) were the signers. All of the signers were just software wallets.
- Early 2023 - As our team and product grew, so did our onchain ops. The 2-of-3 was intended to be our "Treasury", so we created another Gnosis Safe, called "Ops", and made it a 1-of-3. This time all 3 signers were Splits employees (by this point we had incorporated and financed the business), and everyone used company-issued hardware wallets (Ledgers) as signers.
- Late 2023 - L2s started taking off, and our "system of Safes" started to wobble. The addresses didn't match on the new chains, and signing transactions was hard (signers needed the chain's native token for gas). Using our team's onchain assets wasn't even comparable to using Mercury—totally different leagues.
- Early 2024 - We were lucky to be included in Optimism's RPGF, so we had to decide where to receive the funds. The fact that we had to discuss how to receive them was a symptom the system was broken. Where did we lose the plot? We spoke with our friends in similar positions, and everyone felt the same way.
- One week later - [We started laying the foundation for Teams](https://warpcast.com/abram/0x0349e2cb).
