# How we think about contract security

*Last update 10 September 2025*

Ensuring the security of the Splits Ecosystem is our utmost priority. We have invested a significant amount of time and money into designing, testing, and auditing our contracts to maximize their safety and reliability. However, we recognize security is an ongoing process, and the best strategy is one of **layers**. With this in mind, here are our current layers:

- **Simple, immutable, open source code:** We strive for simplicity in our designs and eschew admin overrides and backdoors. Our contracts are [open source](https://github.com/0xSplits/splits-contracts-monorepo/tree/main) and verified on explorers and [Sourcify](https://sourcify.dev/):
  - [Smart Vault Factory](https://etherscan.io/address/0x8e6af8ed94e87b4402d0272c5d6b0d47f0483e7c#code): The factory that deploys Splits' Smart Vaults.
  - [Smart Vault](https://etherscan.io/address/0x15ec0fa66a0d96a64d67c58368641fbe9325f3fa#code): The non-upgradeable implementation of a Splits account. Custodies assets, validates signatures, and executes calls. Your keys, your accounts.
- [**Audits**](https://github.com/0xSplits/splits-contracts-monorepo/blob/main/audits)**:** Before launch, we pay independent security experts to audit our code. At major usage milestones, we hire additional auditors to further review the code.
- [**Bug Bounties**](https://github.com/0xSplits/splits-contracts-monorepo/blob/main/SECURITY.md)**:** After launch, we run a bug bounty program to incentivize independent security experts to continually review our code and safely disclose vulnerabilities. At major usage milestones, we increase our bounties to attract additional scrutiny.
- **Production usage:** Our [splitter contracts](https://dune.com/splits/ecosystem) and [accounts](https://dune.com/splits/teams) are battle-tested with every day and dollar live in production.

If a vulnerability is discovered or reported, our first priority is protecting your funds. We will:

1. Work with [**Seal 911**](https://www.securityalliance.org/seal-911) to whitehat any at-risk funds.
2. Privately notify affected users (email, Telegram, Slack, etc.).
3. Publicly share a post-mortem via our newsletter, blog, and socials.
