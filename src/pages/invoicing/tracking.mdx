# Tracking payments [How invoices get marked paid and attached to transactions]

If someone pays an invoice by clicking *Connect wallet to pay* and then completing the transaction that appears, the invoice will be automatically marked as paid in Splits. Otherwise, you'll need to mark the invoice as paid by clicking the *Mark paid* dropdown on the *Invoices* page:

![](/images/invoicing/img-7.png)

When you do so, you have the option to attach one or more transactions to the invoice for bookkeeping purposes. Click the ones you want to select and hit *Confirm*. Note that if the invoicee used *Connect to pay* on the invoice page, the transaction is automatically attached.

![](/images/invoicing/img-8.png)
