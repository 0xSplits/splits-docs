# Paying invoices [How an invoicee pays — wallet, manual send, or bank]

To pay an invoice, you have a few options.

## Paying with crypto

Tap *Connect wallet to pay* on the invoice page. As soon as you connect your wallet, the transaction will appear on your screen.

You can also manually send funds by copying the payment address just below *Connect wallet to pay*. Make sure to send on the correct network!

![](/images/invoicing/img-5.png)

## Paying via fiat

If bank payments are enabled on the invoice, you'll see a tab called *Bank* which includes the details to send funds directly to the recipient's bank account. Enabling this is covered in [Issuing invoices](/invoicing).

![](/images/invoicing/img-6.png)
