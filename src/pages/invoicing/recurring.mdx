# Recurring invoices [Make an invoice recur on a schedule]

When [issuing an invoice](/invoicing), toggle the *Make recurring* switch on and fill in the details, and your invoice will reoccur on a schedule. If you've entered a customer email, each new invoice will be sent to them.

![](/images/invoicing/img-3.png)

To see and edit recurring invoices after you've created them, go to the *Schedules* tab on the *Invoices* page:

![](/images/invoicing/img-4.png)
