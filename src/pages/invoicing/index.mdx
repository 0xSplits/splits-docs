# Invoicing [Request payment into your accounts, in crypto or by bank transfer]

Invoices let you request payment into your accounts, payable in crypto or, if enabled, by bank transfer. Invoicing is free. This page covers issuing an invoice; see also [Recurring](/invoicing/recurring), [Paying](/invoicing/paying), and [Tracking](/invoicing/tracking).

## Issuing

Head to the *Invoices* tab and click *Create* in the top right corner.

1. Fill out the first block of inputs. To send the invoice to the client by email, add their email here.
2. Select the tokens you want to receive — any token on any network your account has turned on in *Settings*. The receiving account can be any of your accounts, including an [automation](/accounts#automation-accounts)'s deposit address.
3. Pick an issue date and due date, and click *Create*.

## Pay-by-bank

To let the invoicee pay from a bank account, first set up bank transfers in *Settings > Banks*. Then, in the *Create invoice* form, set the token to a valid stablecoin:

- **USDC** on any network if your bank account is US-based
- **EURC** on Mainnet or Base if your bank account is EU-based

Then turn on the *Allow pay by bank* toggle that appears.

:::note
If an invoicee pays by bank, funds flow to the account and network in your **Bank settings**, even if the form specifies a different account or network.
:::
