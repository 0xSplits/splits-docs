# Automations

*Last updated 4 March 2026*

Automations let you create bespoke "deposit addresses" that execute swaps/bridges/etc when tokens are received.

# How Automations work

Under the hood, each Automation is an internal sub-account with a Privy signer. Every 10 minutes, we check the account's balance and, if there is $5 or more of an asset, run your automation policy (splitting, swapping, and forwarding as desired).

# Creating an Automation

Navigate to [Automations](https://app.splits.org/automations/) and click *Create automation* in the top right corner.

Give it a name, specify the tokens and their distribution you want to receive, and select a deposit address (this is where the funds will be sent). Optionally add a memo to attach to each automation transaction.

![](/images/automations/img-1.png)

After creating the automation you can get the newly created account address as highlighted below. This is your deposit address for the Automation. Use it as a receiving address for receiving payments - e.g. as a Splits contracts beneficiary, [invoice payment](/getting-started/invoicing), or anything.

![](/images/automations/img-2.png)

# Viewing and running an Automation

Click on the Automation row to view it's detail page. You can view your current Automation configuration by clicking *Policy.*

![](/images/automations/img-3.png)

You can also edit your Automation configuration by clicking *Edit.* The new policy will be applied to all incoming funds after the update is saved. Click *Trigger* to one-time process any funds currently sitting in your Automation.

# FAQs

**What are the fees?**

There are no fees.

**What networks are supported?**

The same (EVM) networks your accounts are active on. Go to [Settings \> Networks](https://app.splits.org/settings/team/networks/) to see which accounts your team is active on.

**How frequently do these run?**

They run every 10 minutes, as long as there is more than $5 of a token in the account waiting to be processed.

**Technically how does this work?**

Each automation is a 1-of-n multi-signature account where one of the signers is a Privy server key.

**What happens when tokens received are the same as the output tokens?**

They will be forwarded to the destination as is.
