# Invoicing

Onchain invoicing is now flexible, secure, and free. In this guide, we'll walk through the process of creating, sharing, and paying an invoice.

# Create an invoice

Head to the *Invoices* tab in Splits and click *Create* in the top right corner to get started.

Fill out the first block of inputs. If you want the client to receive the invoice via email, add it here:

![](/images/invoicing/img-1.png)

Next, select the tokens you want to receive. You can request any token on any network that your account has turned on in *Settings*. Pick an issue date and due date, and click *Create* to finish.

### Pay by bank

To allow your invoicee to pay with a bank account, you'll need to first set up bank transfers by going to *Settings \> Banks*. Then, in the *Create invoice* form, set the token to a valid stablecoin:

- **USDC** on any network if your bank account is US-based
- **EURC** on Mainnet or Base if your bank account is EU-based

Then, turn on the *Allow pay by bank* toggle that appears.

:::note
Note that if an invoicee pays by bank, funds will flow to the account on the network in your **Bank settings**, even if you specify a different account or network on the form.
:::

![](/images/invoicing/img-2.png)

### Make an invoice recurring

Just toggle the *Make recurring* switch on, fill in a few details, and your invoice will reoccur on a schedule. If you've entered a customer email, each new invoice will be sent to them.

![](/images/invoicing/img-3.png)

To see and edit recurring invoices after you've created them, go to the *Schedules* tab on the *Invoices* page:

![](/images/invoicing/img-4.png)

# Pay an invoice

To pay an invoice, you have a few options. The easiest is to just tap *Connect wallet to pay* on the invoice page. As soon as you connect your wallet, the transaction will appear on your screen.

You can also manually send funds by copying the payment address just below *Connect wallet to pay*. Make sure to send on the correct network!

Finally, if bank payments are enabled, you'll see a tab called *Bank* which includes the details to send funds directly to the recipient's bank account.

![](/images/invoicing/img-5.png)

![](/images/invoicing/img-6.png)

# Track payments

If someone pays an invoice by clicking *Connect wallet to pay* and then completing the transaction that appears, the invoice will be automatically marked as paid in Splits. Otherwise, you'll need to mark the invoice as paid by clicking *Mark paid* dropdown on the *Invoices* page:

![](/images/invoicing/img-7.png)

When you do so, you have the option to attach one or more transactions to the invoice for bookkeeping purposes. Just click the ones you want to select, and hit *Confirm*. Note that if the invoicee used *Connect to pay* on the invoice page, the transaction is automatically attached.

![](/images/invoicing/img-8.png)
