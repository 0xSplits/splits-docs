# Batch transactions

*Last updated June 9 2026*

Splits offers native transaction batching. Instead of creating and signing a list of transactions one by one, you can add them to a batch and sign them all at once. Batching works anywhere in the Splits app, as well as in external apps using our [Chrome extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/splits/ghfacfafnbcgkielpaeifdpoggfeakif) or WalletConnect.

### Batching in our app

To get started, prepare a transaction in Splits. When you reach the review dialog, click the dropdown to the right of *Submit* and then click *Add to batch*.

![](/images/batch-transactions/img-1.png)

Continue adding transactions until you're ready to sign them all. When you're done, you can find the batch at the top of the transaction feed on the dashboard or corresponding account page:

![](/images/batch-transactions/img-2.png)

At the top of the dialog, you'll see a collapsible list of transactions in the batch. You can inspect the calls being made, see a Tenderly simulation, or remove any transaction in the batch.

![](/images/batch-transactions/img-3.png)

![](/images/batch-transactions/img-4.png)

When you're finished adding transactions, click *Submit* and sign the transaction.

:::note
Note: currently, each organization can have one active batch at a time, on one account and chain. You'll see a disabled *Add to batch* button if yours has an active batch already.
:::

### Batching in other apps

You can also use Splits Connect or WalletConnect to add transactions from external apps to a batch. You'll see a similar dropdown when reviewing transactions in external apps.

![](/images/batch-transactions/img-5.png)

When you see the confirmation below, the transaction was successfully added to your batch. Note that external apps in this case may not display the transaction as pending in their UI.

![](/images/batch-transactions/img-6.png)
