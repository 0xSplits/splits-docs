# Showing/hiding tokens (spam)

*Last updated 30 August 2025*

Spam is a big problem in the industry so we're working hard to ensure you only see the tokens that are relevant for your team. We have some basic filtering working for you, but there will always be instances where you need to customize which tokens are displayed for your team.

# Turning on specific tokens

If you're not seeing a token you should be, navigate to [Settings > Tokens](https://app.splits.org/settings/team/tokens/) to see a list of all tokens held in your accounts. The switch on the right controls whether that token is displayed in the app for your team. On means displayed; off means hidden. Hidden tokens will not show up in your balances, transaction list, or accounting page.

![](/images/showing-hiding-tokens/img-1.png)

# Hiding tokens

If you find tokens showing that shouldn't be, instead of navigating to the Settings page and searching for the token each time, you can quickly mark a token as spam from the transaction view.

![](/images/showing-hiding-tokens/img-2.png)

Hover over a transaction, click the three dots menu, and select *Report spam.* This will turn off the token and prevent it from being displayed in the app for anyone on your team.
