# Connecting to other apps

*Last updated 08 May 2026*

Install our browser extension, [Splits Connect](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/splits/ghfacfafnbcgkielpaeifdpoggfeakif), to connect your accounts to third-party apps.

If you use a non-Chromium browser (e.g. Safari), [WalletConnect](#using-walletconnect) also works.

Download the latest version of [Splits Connect](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/splits/ghfacfafnbcgkielpaeifdpoggfeakif) extension.

Once the extension is installed, just open the third party app and select Splits as the wallet.

![](/images/connecting-to-other-apps/img-1.png)

Select the team and account you want to connect and hit *Connect*.

![](/images/connecting-to-other-apps/img-2.png)

Once you click *Connect* the dialog will close on its own and the account will be displayed on the third party app.

Now, when you perform a transaction, the dialog will open and you can use your passkey to sign and submit the transaction.

# Known limitations

Not all third party apps support [EIP-712](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-712), which means you'll have to use WalletConnect on these apps.
