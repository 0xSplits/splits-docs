# Introduction [What Splits is, who it's for, and how to start]

**Splits is the onchain operations platform for builders.** Manage assets, process revenue, move money, and run operations instantly, via [the app](https://app.splits.org) or [API/CLI](/introduction/agents).

Key properties:

- **Self-custodied.** Signers are passkeys or EOAs held by your people — Splits never has access to private keys and cannot move your funds.
- **Isolated by account.** Create as many [accounts](/accounts) as you need — each one is a labeled sub-ledger with its own signers and thresholds, isolating a revenue source, program, campaign, partner, or temporary operation while everything stays in one workspace.
- **Cross-chain by default.** Every account has the same address on all [supported networks](/introduction/networks-and-assets), and assets can be [swapped and bridged](/transactions/swaps) across chains in a single transaction.
- **Integrated fiat.** Move between tokens and fiat — [on/offramps](/banking) to your bank accounts, invoices [payable by bank transfer](/invoicing#pay-by-bank), and [vendor payments](/banking/paying-vendors).
- **Composable.** Connect your multi-signature accounts to any app in the ecosystem via the [browser extension](/introduction/extension) or WalletConnect.
- **Professional workflows.** [Accounting](/accounting) with filters and exports, [invoicing](/invoicing), [bill pay](/banking/paying-vendors), payroll via [schedules](/transactions/schedules), [tax withholding](/workflows/auto-withhold-for-taxes), and compliant payments (collect W9s/W8s, issue 1099s).
- **Recoverable.** Recovery signers can regain control of everything if passkeys are lost.
- **Agent-ready.** Everything is scriptable via the [CLI and MCP](/introduction/agents), and every docs page is available as markdown — append `.md` to any URL, or fetch `/llms.txt` / `/llms-full.txt`.

## Who it's for

- **Teams and companies operating onchain** — shared treasury with per-account signer sets, roles, and approvals.
- **Solo builders** — the same setup works for [teams of one](/introduction/personal-usage): resilient multisigs, clean books, separation of project and personal funds.
- **Agents** — AI tools operating on a team's behalf via API keys, with scoped permissions and optional headless signing.
