# Thresholds [The number of signer approvals for a transaction]

A **threshold** is the number of [signer](/accounts/signers) approvals an [account](/accounts) requires for a transaction.

The notation is M-of-N. For example, 2-of-3 requires any two of three signers. A transaction stays pending until it has M signatures.

Each account has its own threshold. Team setup sets the Treasury's initial threshold. Account creation sets an operating account's initial threshold. [Editing](/accounts/editing) describes later changes.

<a id="choosing-a-threshold" />

## Threshold examples

During team setup, the app suggests a majority of Owners for the Treasury.

| Owners | Treasury | Recovery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1-of-1 | 2-of-3 |
| 2 | 2-of-2 | 2-of-3 |
| 3 | 2-of-3 | 2-of-3 |
| 4+ | Majority | Majority |

A **majority** means more than half of the configured signers.

[Recovery](/teams/recovery) describes recovery signers. [Personal usage](/introduction/personal-usage#multisigs-of-one) describes multiple signers controlled by one person.
