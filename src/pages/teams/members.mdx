# Members [Who's in a team, their roles, and how membership differs from signing authority]

A **team member** is a person who belongs to a [team](/teams). Membership is stored offchain and grants read access to the team's accounts, balances, and transactions, plus administrative capabilities determined by the member's role.

Membership confers **no onchain authority**: a member cannot move funds unless one of their [signers](/accounts/signers) has been added to an account's signer set.

## Roles

Every member has exactly one of two roles: **Owner** or **Member**.

- **Owners** administer the team (members, accounts, invoices, automations, banking, recovery) and can propose transactions from any account.
- **Members** can propose transactions only from accounts they're a signer on, and cannot access Owner-only settings.

For the full capability matrix, see [Permissions](/teams/permissions).

## Members vs signers

The two often overlap 1:1, but are distinct:

| | Team member | Signer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stored | Offchain | Onchain (account's signer set) |
| Scoped to | A team | An account |
| What it is | A person's association with a team | A key belonging to a member |
| Grants | Visibility + role capabilities | Authority to approve transactions, counted against the account's threshold |
| Managed in | Settings > Members | Account settings > Signers ([how](/accounts/thresholds#changing-signers-and-thresholds)) |

Two rules connect them:

1. **Every signer on a team's accounts must be a member of that team.**
2. **Membership alone never makes someone a signer.** Adding a signer is a separate, onchain action.

## Read-only members

A member with the **Member** role who isn't a signer on any account is effectively read-only: they can see the team's accounts, balances, and transactions, but can't propose or sign any. (This requires the Member role. Owners can propose transactions from any account.)

To set one up, invite them with the Member role and don't add them to any account's signer set. Useful for accountants, auditors, and anyone who needs visibility without the ability to move funds.

## Inviting

Prerequisites: you must be an **Owner** of the team.

1. Go to [Settings > Members](https://app.splits.org/settings/team/members/) and select *Invite member*.
2. Enter the invitee's email address and select their role.
3. The invitee receives an email with a join link (it may land in spam). If they're new to Splits, they create a person account as part of accepting.
4. During acceptance, the invitee is prompted to save a passkey: they need at least one [signer](/accounts/signers) before they can be added to an account's signer set.
5. You receive an email confirmation once they've accepted.

To give the new member signing power on an account, continue to [Managing signers](/accounts/thresholds#changing-signers-and-thresholds).

## Role changes and removal

Owners can change a member's role, remove a member, or revoke a pending invitation from the member's row in [Settings > Members](https://app.splits.org/settings/team/members/).

Removing a member revokes their offchain access only. If their signers are on any account, remove them via a separate onchain [signer update](/accounts/thresholds#changing-signers-and-thresholds).

## Programmatic access

Via the [Splits CLI / MCP](/introduction/agents):

- `splits members list`: list all members of the team (requires **Read** scope)
- `splits members signers <userId>`: list a member's passkey IDs, needed when adding them as a signer via `accounts create` or `accounts update-signers` (requires **Read** scope)

Managing members (inviting, removing, changing roles) is web-only today.
