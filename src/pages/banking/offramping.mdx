# Offramping [Move crypto out to the workspace's bank accounts by Same Day ACH or SEPA]

An **offramp** is a crypto to bank account transfer: the account sends crypto, and the bank receives fiat. You can offramp any token, such as ETH. The app first sells any token other than the payout stablecoin (such as USDC) at spot. That sale pays the workspace tier's [swap fee](https://splits.org/pricing/), on top of the 0.25% [banking fee](/banking). In the US, offramps go by Same Day ACH. In the EU, they go by SEPA.

Before you can offramp, add a bank account from [Settings > Banks](https://app.splits.org/settings/team/banks/): connect a US account with [Plaid](https://plaid.com/) or enter its routing and account numbers manually; EU accounts are added by IBAN. You can add as many bank accounts as you wish.

To offramp, open *Send* and choose your bank account as the recipient. You can also go to Settings > Banks, click the three dots on the target bank account, and select *Offramp*; this opens *Send* with that bank account selected.

## Which bank accounts can receive an offramp?

An offramp goes to a business or personal bank account in the name of the workspace's verified entity: a business after KYB, an individual after KYC. See [Getting verified](/banking#getting-verified). To offramp to someone else's bank account, see [Paying vendors](/banking/paying-vendors).

## Exact bank amount

Enter the fiat amount you want the bank to receive in *Recipient gets*. The app increases the token amount sent to cover the 0.25% [banking fee](/banking), so the bank receives the amount entered. For example, to receive $1,995.00, the account sends 2,000.00 USDC: $5.00 covers the fee. For a token other than the payout stablecoin, such as ETH, the account sells it at spot first, and that sale pays the swap fee too.

The review step shows the amount sent, the fee, and the amount the bank receives before you confirm. The minimum amount the bank can receive is $10 or €10.

## Settlement

In the US, we send via [Same Day ACH, which settles in batches throughout the day](https://apidocs.bridge.xyz/platform/orchestration/more/cutoffs#processing-windows-and-cutoff-times). In Eastern Time, settlement occurs at 1:00 pm, 5:00 pm, and 6:00 pm, with corresponding cutoffs at 9:15 am, 1:30 pm, and 3:30 pm (we've added a 15m buffer relative to Bridge's documentation, which has been necessary in our experience). ACH offramps sent after 3:30 pm ET arrive the following business day.

For IBAN banks supporting SEPA Instant, settlement occurs in under 30 seconds.

## Using your own provider

If your workspace already has an offramping provider, you can offramp through it instead, without completing Splits' [entity verification](/banking#getting-verified). Most providers issue a **liquidation address**: a deposit address that converts incoming stablecoins to fiat and forwards them to your bank account. Save that address as a [contact](/contacts), then [send](/transactions/sends) stablecoins to it like any other recipient.

Splits treats these as ordinary sends: the 0.25% banking fee and the settlement times above don't apply; your provider's own fees and timing do.

## Programmatic access

Via the [Splits CLI / MCP](/introduction/agents):

- `splits offramp external-accounts`: the workspace's bank accounts (**Owner** scope)
- `splits transactions create offramp --account 0x... --external-account-id <id> --amount 100`: propose an off-ramp of the amount the bank receives (**Write** and **Owner** scopes)

Adding bank accounts and onramping are web-only today.
