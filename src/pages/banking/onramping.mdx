# Onramping [Move fiat into your accounts by ACH or wire]

Once your entity is [verified](/banking#getting-verified), you can onramp funds to your team via ACH or wire. Click *Onramp* from the Dashboard, or find the deposit details in [Settings > Banks](https://app.splits.org/settings/team/banks/).

By default, funds arrive in your Treasury account as USDC on Base. You can change the destination account and network at any time.

Onramps carry the 0.25% [banking fee](/banking), deducted from the amount.
